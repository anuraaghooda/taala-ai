The Problem

One undocumented gap can cost more than your entire compliance budget.

Most teams deploying AI can't prove where their training data came from, whether they own their outputs, or which rules apply to them. That gap stays invisible until it becomes a fine, a lost deal, or a lawsuit.

⚠️

Stalled Enterprise Deals

Enterprise buyers increasingly ask where your training data came from. If you can't answer, procurement stalls and the deal goes to a competitor who can.

©️

Copyright Litigation

Courts are actively ruling on AI training and outputs. Finding exposure yourself costs a review; having a rights holder find it costs a lawsuit.

🌐

Regulatory Fines

EU AI Act penalties reach up to €35M or 7% of global annual turnover for the most serious violations, and the Act can apply to US companies whose AI is used in the EU. Knowing which obligations apply to you is the first step.

🔍

Failed Audits & Due Diligence

Investors, acquirers, and auditors expect documentation you may not have. Gaps found late delay rounds, reprice deals, and cost far more to fix under pressure.
